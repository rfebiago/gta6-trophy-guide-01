Roadmap to the platinum

PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 and about 200 hours to the platinum, in a single playthrough. There are no difficulty settings, no mission gold medals, and no mission replay — so keep a manual save at the start of each chapter (the game gives you 15 manual save slots). Cheats switch trophies and saving off, so keep them off on your trophy save. The 100% checklist is lenient — PowerPyx calls it roughly 60% of true completion — and nothing on it is missable.

    About this guide

    • Official list. Names, descriptions and grades are the 55 trophies as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles, reported by Push Square, GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer. Rockstar, Sony and Microsoft have not published the list themselves. Shown here exactly as the lists carry them.
    • Own words, cited sources. Every guide is written for this page and names its sources beside it — mostly the PowerPyx trophy guide and roadmap, with KeenGamer, Output Lag and GTA Zone corroborating the list. A specific location or step with no source earns no trust here.
    • Honest gaps. Five trophies were still unconfirmed at launch (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin). Their guides say so instead of inventing a method. Check the linked PowerPyx guide for updates.
    • No online trophies. All 55 are single-player and offline. The Online filter exists so you can see that for yourself — it finds nothing, by design.
    • Your data stays yours. Progress lives in this browser's local storage. Export writes a file you own; import reads only the file you choose. Nothing is sent anywhere — this page makes no network requests at all.
    • Not affiliated. An unofficial fan guide. Grand Theft Auto and Rockstar Games are trademarks of their owners; no Rockstar logo, artwork, or game imagery is used here.
